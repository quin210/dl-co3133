Conclusion

  • Inductive bias over size: a 25,337-parameter CNN (90.51%) beats the 203,530-parameter MLP (88.77%).
  • Hard cases in the data: Shirt is the weakest class in all 25 runs, and 146 test images are wrong in every run.
  • Different failure modes: CNN is the most robust to shift, occlusion and contrast, but noise reverses the ranking; the sequence direction decides which shift hurts LSTM.
  • Sensitivity, not reasons: explanations depend on the seed for every model except Linear; Transformer attention is not shorter-range on average than uniform attention.
  • The sequence matters: GRU is 0.78 pp better than LSTM with 24% fewer parameters, and row tokens make the Transformer 1.38 pp better (both 5/5 seeds).
  • Robustness has a price: shift + noise augmentation repairs the shift weakness but lowers clean macro-F1 in 5/5 seeds (−0.92 pp CNN to −5.66 pp Linear).
